# Lettuce

![[localdata/plant_images/03-lettuce.jpg]]

## Summary

A quick leafy crop with varieties for each season.

## Sowing

All year

## Care

Grow in full sun or part shade in rich, well-drained soil; keep evenly moist.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 head per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Quick leafy food crop.
Fresh leaves for salads, sandwiches and wraps.
