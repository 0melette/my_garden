# Tomato - Black Krim

![[localdata/plant_images/33-tomato-black-krim.jpg]]

## Summary

Dark slicing tomato for warm-season growing with a sturdy stake or cage.

## Sowing

Aug–Dec

## Care

Provide a sturdy stake or cage and tie stems loosely as the plant grows.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

3 kg per established plant over a 10-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Tall summer crop; keep lower-growing companions outside its main root and shade area.
Fresh in salads, roasted, or cooked into sauces and relishes.
