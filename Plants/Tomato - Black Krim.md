# Tomato - Black Krim

![[localdata/plant_images/33-tomato-black-krim.jpg]]

## Summary

Dark slicing tomato for warm-season growing with a sturdy stake or cage.

## Sowing

Aug–Dec

## Care

Scope: General tomato care; named-cultivar traits and exact watering intervals are not verified. Sources checked 20 October 2026. UK/US calendar dates are not Sydney planting dates.

Sunlight: Choose full sun and a sheltered position. [RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own)

Watering: Keep the root zone evenly moist; water deeply during dry weather. Check containers frequently because they dry faster. Adjust watering to soil moisture, rainfall and heat rather than a fixed weekly schedule. [Maryland Extension](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-tomatoes-home-garden)

Fertiliser: Base additional feeding on soil fertility, growth and fruit load. For containers, RHS recommends high-potassium liquid feed every 10–14 days once fruit begins swelling. Follow the product label for dilution; this is not a universal bed-feeding schedule. [RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own) · [Maryland Extension](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-tomatoes-home-garden)

Support: Support tall plants and tie stems loosely. [RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own)

Pruning: Confirm the growth habit on the packet or label first. When training indeterminate tomatoes as a single cordon, remove side shoots; retain side shoots on determinate bush tomatoes. Do not apply cordon pruning to an unidentified cultivar. [RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own)

Unknowns: A cultivar name, water-needs category or heavy-feeder label alone does not establish watering frequency, fertiliser dose or pruning method.

## Management

Prevention: Water at soil level, maintain airflow and rotate crops to reduce disease pressure. [Maryland Extension](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-tomatoes-home-garden)

Checks: Record which leaves or fruits are affected, spot patterns, moisture conditions and visible pests before selecting a response. Early blight can affect tomato foliage and fruit; a catalogue association is not a diagnosis. [Maryland Extension](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-tomatoes-home-garden)

Related reference: [[References/Diseases/Tomato early blight]]. Treatment and cultivar resistance are not established by this care record.

## Harvest

Planning estimate, not a verified Black Krim yield: 3 kg per established plant over a 10-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Tall summer crop; keep lower-growing companions outside its main root and shade area.
Fresh in salads, roasted, or cooked into sauces and relishes.
