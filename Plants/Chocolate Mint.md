# Chocolate Mint

![[localdata/plant_images/41-chocolate-mint.jpg]]

## Summary

Vigorous perennial mint grown for aromatic leaves with a chocolate-mint scent.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: divide or plant September–November or March–April. These months translate spring/early-autumn propagation advice to local seasons. Use divisions or cuttings to retain the cultivar.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Grow in sun or light shade with moist, drained soil. Keep in a pot or root barrier. Allow 60 cm per plant and 65 cm between rows as a contained-bed planning layout; uncontained roots spread farther. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, soil ph min, soil ph max, feeder type, water needs.

## Management

Keep runners contained and avoid overhead watering late in the day. Check for rust and remove affected foliage. pH 6–7.5 is a general herb target, not a measured Chocolate cultivar tolerance.

## Harvest

Harvest established shoot tips regularly for regrowth. One contained plant can supply repeated small pickings. No measured per-plant yield or fixed harvest duration is available.

## Uses

Use aromatic leaves in teas, desserts and drinks, fresh or dried. Pinching tips encourages a bushier plant.
