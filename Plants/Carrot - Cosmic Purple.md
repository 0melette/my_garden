# Carrot - Cosmic Purple

![[localdata/plant_images/14-carrot-cosmic-purple.jpg]]

## Summary

Fast-growing carrot with smooth purple skin, near core-less orange interior and sweet roots to about 18cm.

## Sowing

Spring, summer and autumn

## Care

Direct sow into well-cultivated soil; thin to final spacing.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 root per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Direct-sown root crop.
Sweet and crunchy; retains colour well when cooked.
