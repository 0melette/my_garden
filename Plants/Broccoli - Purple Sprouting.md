# Broccoli - Purple Sprouting

![[localdata/plant_images/12-broccoli-purple-sprouting.jpg]]

## Summary

Purple sprouting broccoli with green stalks and small to medium purple heads; continues producing side shoots after picking.

## Sowing

Late summer to autumn

## Care

Sow direct or raise seedlings. Cut heads before flowers open. Protect from aphids and caterpillars.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

0.6 kg per established plant over a 6-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Long-season cool-weather food crop.
Good for stir-frying or steaming; purple colour reduces when cooked.
