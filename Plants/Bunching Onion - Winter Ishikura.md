# Bunching Onion - Winter Ishikura

![[localdata/plant_images/13-bunching-onion-winter-ishikura.jpg]]

## Summary

Hardy bunching onion with long white bases and green tops to about 45cm; used similarly to spring onions.

## Sowing

Mar-Nov

## Care

Sow direct or raise seedlings; hardy and suitable for cool-season production.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 plant per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Compact cool-season allium and repeat-harvest kitchen crop.
Good raw or cooked wherever spring onions are used.
