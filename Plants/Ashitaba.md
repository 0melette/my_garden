## Summary

Ashitaba (*Angelica keiskei*), also called tomorrow leaf, is a leafy herb in the carrot family (Apiaceae). NParks describes it as biennial or perennial, reaching up to 1.5 m, with toothed leaves and clusters of small white flowers.

## Sowing

A Sydney sowing calendar and seed-treatment method have not yet been established for this entry.

## Care

Species guidance: NParks recommends moist, well-drained soil and semi-shade in Singapore. The part-shade and moderate-water properties follow that source; its tropical growing context does not establish a Sydney watering schedule. [NParks species profile](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/6/3/6399), checked 3 November 2026.
