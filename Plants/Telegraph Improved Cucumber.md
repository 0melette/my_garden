# Telegraph Improved Cucumber

![[localdata/plant_images/08-telegraph-improved-cucumber.jpg]]

## Summary

A heavy-cropping cucumber with smooth, thin skin and a sweet, crisp flavour. The long fruit is well suited to salads, sandwiches and tzatziki.
