# Bean - Lazy Housewife

![[localdata/plant_images/37-bean-lazy-housewife.jpg]]

## Summary

Climbing bean with stringless young pods, grown on a sturdy support.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: direct sow September–January after frost, 2–3 cm deep. A 21-day repeat sowing is an optional planning interval within that season, interpreting guidance to sow every few weeks.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Install a trellis before sowing. Space plants 20 cm apart in rows 100 cm apart. Keep moist after emergence without waterlogging; avoid excessive nitrogen. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, succession interval days, water needs.

## Management

Protect seedlings from slugs and snails. Water the soil rather than foliage. Nitrogen fixation depends on compatible soil bacteria and does not guarantee feeding nearby crops.

## Harvest

The supplier gives about 80 days to first harvest. Pick young pods regularly for a continuing crop; no reliable kilogram yield per plant is verified.

## Uses

Harvest tender green pods for cooking. Let selected pods mature fully only if saving seed or harvesting dry beans; cook mature beans thoroughly.
