# Musk Mallow

## Summary

Daleys lists this Musk Mallow as Abelmoschus moschatus tuberosus, also called Native Rosella. It has red flowers and musk-scented seeds. This record is not Malva moschata, another plant called musk mallow.

## Sowing

Daleys supplies seedling-grown plants. Local sowing months are not recorded.

## Care

Scope: Supplier species listing, checked 20 October 2026.

Sunlight: Full sun. Daleys lists drought tolerance and only occasional frost tolerance. These categories do not establish an establishment watering schedule or a safe minimum temperature.

Size: The description says about 2 m, while the specifications say 0–1 m. The supplier's conflicting height information needs confirmation before spacing decisions.
