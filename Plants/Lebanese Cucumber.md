# Lebanese Cucumber

![[localdata/plant_images/07-lebanese-cucumber.jpg]]

## Summary

A sweet-tasting, burpless and high-yielding cucumber with excellent flavour. Best picked young and tender at about 10–15 cm long.

## Sowing

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): Sow in warm soil after frost risk. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucumis-sativus/).

## Care

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): Watering: Maintain consistent soil moisture with good drainage. Support: A trellis suits climbing growth. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucumis-sativus/).

## Management

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): Water soil rather than foliage. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucumis-sativus/).

## Harvest

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): Pick green fruit frequently before it yellows. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucumis-sativus/).

## Uses

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): Edible fruit. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucumis-sativus/).
