# Tomato - Sweet 1000

![[localdata/plant_images/39-tomato-sweet-1000.jpg]]

## Summary

Tomato recorded as Sweet 1000. The cultivar name is listed by a supplier; the growing guidance here is for tomatoes generally until the seed-packet identity is confirmed.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: sow August–December using general tomato guidance from Eden’s mild-climate calendar. Start early sowings under protection and transplant after frost risk. Not a verified Sweet 1000 calendar.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. General tomato guidance: provide full sun, steady soil moisture and support appropriate to the plant’s habit. Allow 90 cm between plants and 150 cm between rows as a roomy planning layout. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, soil ph min, soil ph max, feeder type, water needs, sun needs, forest layer.

## Management

Mulch, water evenly and allow airflow. Rotate away from other nightshade crops. Spacing, feeding, water, pH, layer and calendar are species-level guidance, not verified cultivar traits.

## Harvest

Pick ripe fruit as it develops. Generic tomato guidance suggests about 65–90 days from transplanting to first harvest, but Sweet 1000 maturity, crop duration and yield remain unverified.

## Uses

Use ripe tomato fruit fresh or cooked. The record is kept separate from Sweet 100 and Sweet Million; fruit size and flavour are not assumed.
