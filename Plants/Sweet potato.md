# Sweet potato

![[localdata/plant_images/32-sweet-potato.jpg]]

## Summary

Vigorous warm-season groundcover crop grown for edible tubers and young leaf tips.

## Sowing

Warm frost-free months

## Care

Plant in prepared soil rich in organic matter; water deeply during dry weather.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 kg per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Spreading warm-season food crop.
Roast, bake, mash or add to curries and soups.
