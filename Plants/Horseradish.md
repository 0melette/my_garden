# Horseradish

![[localdata/plant_images/20-horseradish.jpg]]

## Summary

Deep-rooted perennial vegetable grown for its pungent edible root; best contained because small root pieces can regrow.

## Sowing

Late autumn–early spring

## Care

Grow in full sun or light shade in moist soil, preferably in a large container to limit spread.

## Management

Shallow rooted plants around the edge ot he container

## Harvest

0.3 kg per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Deep-rooted perennial crop; shallow-rooted plants can occupy the container edge.
Freshly grated horseradish with vinegar and a pinch of salt makes a classic sauce.
