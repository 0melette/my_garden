# Grass Jelly

![[localdata/plant_images/19-grass-jelly.jpg]]

## Summary

Tropical climbing plant traditionally used to make green grass jelly in Indonesia; confirm identity and food safety before use.

## Sowing

Propagate from seed or underground storage organs; a Sydney sowing calendar is not established here. [Source](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/5/5/5585).

## Care

Grow with a trellis or other support under light shade; cultivation guidance is limited.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

The leaves are used in green grass jelly. This source gives no harvest interval or yield. [Source](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/5/5/5585).

## Uses

Climbing tropical plant with traditional culinary and medicinal associations.
Traditional grass jelly preparation; confirm species identity and food safety before use.
