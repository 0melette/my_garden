# Bergamot - Lemon Mint

![[localdata/plant_images/11-bergamot-lemon-mint.jpg]]

## Summary

Lemon-scented annual herb to about 60cm with lavender flowers; also called Lemon Bee Balm; attracts bees and suits borders.

## Sowing

Jan-Apr, Sep-Dec

## Care

Raise seedlings; protect from slugs, snails and powdery mildew.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

Use harvest_window_weeks as a rough picking/lifting period; yield_qty is a total per established plant per crop/season, not per picking. Harvest at suitable maturity and adjust after observing your plants.

## Uses

Flowering border herb that attracts bees.
Leaves can be used to flavour poultry and fish dishes.
