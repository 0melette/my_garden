# Pea - Greenfeast

![[localdata/plant_images/26-pea-greenfeast.jpg]]

## Summary

Cool-season shelling pea; pick pods while the peas are young and tender.

## Sowing

Feb–Aug

## Care

Provide a pea frame or trellis and pick pods regularly.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

0.25 kg per established plant over a 4-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Cool-season legume and vertical layer when grown on support.
Fresh or cooked in soups, risotto and side dishes.
