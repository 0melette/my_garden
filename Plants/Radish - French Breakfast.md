# Radish - French Breakfast

![[localdata/plant_images/28-radish-french-breakfast.jpg]]

## Summary

Quick-growing radish suited to small, repeated sowings.

## Sowing

Feb–Nov

## Care

Sow small batches regularly and harvest while roots are young.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 root per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Quick root crop for succession sowing.
Raw in salads, pickled, or roasted.
