# Eggplant - Tsakoniki

![[localdata/plant_images/18-eggplant-tsakoniki.jpg]]

## Summary

Greek variety producing attractive purple-and-white striped cylindrical fruit to about 22cm; mild firm flesh. Often grown as an annual in cooler climates.

## Sowing

Jan-Feb, Sep-Dec

## Care

Sow direct or raise seedlings. Frost tender. Pick regularly to encourage more fruit; may need staking.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

8 fruit per established plant over a 10-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Warm-season fruiting crop.
Good for stews, sauces, baking, frying, stuffing and grilling.
