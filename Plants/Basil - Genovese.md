# Basil - Genovese

![[localdata/plant_images/10-basil-genovese.jpg]]

## Summary

Sweet basil with aromatic leaves for the kitchen and flowers for visiting insects.

## Sowing

Sep–Feb

## Care

Pinch growing tips to encourage branching and harvest before cold weather.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

0.2 kg per established plant over a 8-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.

## Uses

Culinary herb for the herbaceous layer; allow a few stems to flower for visiting insects.
Fresh leaves for pesto, pasta, salads and sauces.
