# Bean - Scarlet Runner

![[localdata/plant_images/38-bean-scarlet-runner.jpg]]

## Summary

Climbing bean with scarlet flowers and long pods; can regrow from perennial roots in suitable conditions.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: sow November–February, interpreting the Australian supplier’s late-spring to late-summer window. Wait for warm soil; hot spells can reduce pod set.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Provide a strong support and moist, compost-rich soil. The 20 cm plant / 100 cm row spacing and pH 6–7 are general climbing-bean planning targets, not cultivar measurements. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, soil ph min, soil ph max, harvest window weeks, water needs.

## Management

Watch for aphids, slugs and snails. Keep roots moist through flowering. Nitrogen fixation relies on soil bacteria; flowering plants may still need potassium, especially in pots.

## Harvest

Pick tender pods every few days. Eight weeks is a general runner-bean harvest-window baseline, not a Sydney yield guarantee. Perennial roots may regrow in suitable conditions.

## Uses

Grow for scarlet flowers and edible pods. Cook beans before eating; harvest pods young before strings and mature seeds develop.
