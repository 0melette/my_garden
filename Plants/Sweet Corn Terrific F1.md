# Sweet Corn Terrific F1

![[localdata/plant_images/31-sweet-corn-terrific-f1.jpg]]

## Summary

Early-cropping sweet corn with traditional sweet-corn flavour. Best grown in a block for pollination.

## Sowing

Early spring to summer

## Care

Direct sow in blocks rather than a single row; keep moist, water regularly in hot weather and fertilise well. For succession, sow the next crop when the first plants are about 20cm tall.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 cob per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Warm-season crop grown in a block for pollination.
Harvest fresh when silks have browned and kernels release a milky liquid when pierced.
