# Carrot - Solar Yellow

![[localdata/plant_images/16-carrot-solar-yellow.jpg]]

## Summary

Yellow-rooted carrot growing to about 18cm; sweet, juicy and very crunchy.

## Sowing

Spring, summer and autumn

## Care

Direct sow into cultivated soil and thin to final spacing.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 root per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Direct-sown root crop.
Fresh eating or cooked as a colourful carrot variety.
