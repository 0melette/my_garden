# Alyssum

![[localdata/plant_images/09-alyssum.jpg]]

## Summary

Low-growing sweet alyssum used as an edging or groundcover flower that supports pollinators.

## Sowing

Aug–May

## Care

Keep soil moist but not wet while establishing; container plants may need additional fertiliser.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Uses
