# Vietnamese Mint

![[localdata/plant_images/40-vietnamese-mint.jpg]]

## Summary

Spreading perennial herb also called Vietnamese coriander or rau ram, with aromatic leaves used in cooking.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: plant rooted cuttings September–February, translating the Australian nursery’s spring/summer propagation guidance. Protect from cold.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Use a warm, sheltered position with dappled shade and fertile, consistently moist soil. Keep in a container to manage spreading stems. A 45 cm plant / 60 cm row layout is a provisional space allowance. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, feeder type, water needs.

## Management

Prevent drying out and keep spreading stems out of neighbouring pots. RHS lists acid, neutral and alkaline soils without a numeric range, so exact pH limits are not invented. Water/feed badges are qualitative.

## Harvest

Snip fresh leaves and tender tips once established; allow regrowth between pickings. Repeat harvest depends on warmth and vigour, so a fixed weight or harvest duration is not assigned.

## Uses

Use small amounts of fresh peppery leaves in salads, rice-paper rolls and Vietnamese dishes. This is Persicaria odorata, not a Mentha mint.
