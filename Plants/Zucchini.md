# Zucchini

![[localdata/plant_images/06-zucchini.jpg]]

## Summary

A vigorous warm-season crop that produces generously.

## Sowing

Sow into warm soil after frost risk. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucurbita-pepo/).

## Care

Watering: Maintain moderately moist soil. Mulch helps retain soil moisture. Guidance is for Cucurbita pepo; growth habit varies by cultivar. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucurbita-pepo/).

## Management

Monitor for slugs, aphids and beetles. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucurbita-pepo/).

## Harvest

Use harvest_window_weeks as a rough picking/lifting period; yield_qty is a total per established plant per crop/season, not per picking. Harvest at suitable maturity and adjust after observing your plants.

## Uses

Edible fruit. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cucurbita-pepo/).
