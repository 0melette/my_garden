# Marigold - French Marigold

![[localdata/plant_images/25-marigold-french-marigold.jpg]]

## Summary

Compact flowering plant for sunny bed edges and borders.

## Sowing

Sep-Feb

## Care

Deadhead spent flowers and keep in a sunny position.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

This is ornamental/timber planning, with no edible-harvest target. Do not treat flowers, shoots or other parts as safe food based on this record.

## Uses

Flowering edge plant for a varied insect-friendly border.
Primarily ornamental; do not eat unless the packet confirms an edible cultivar.
