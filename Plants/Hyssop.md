# Hyssop

![[localdata/plant_images/21-hyssop.jpg]]

## Summary

Evergreen aromatic herb to about 50cm with violet-blue flower spikes; culinary and medicinal use; attracts bees and butterflies.

## Sowing

Jan-Feb, Sep-Dec

## Care

Raise seedlings; transplant at 5-10cm tall. Water deeply when soil is dry about 5cm down. Prune after flowering / in spring.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Uses

Aromatic perennial herb that attracts bees and butterflies.
Strong bitter-minty flavour; used in soups, stews, salads, meat and egg dishes.
