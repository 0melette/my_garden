# Sunflower - Golden Prominence F1

![[localdata/plant_images/30-sunflower-golden-prominence-f1.jpg]]

## Summary

Tall pollen-free sunflower with a single large golden-yellow flower, deep red inner ring and dark centre; good for beds, borders and cut flowers.

## Sowing

Spring and early summer

## Care

Sow direct or raise seedlings. Water deeply at less frequent intervals. Hybrid F1 seed will not grow true to type if saved.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 flower per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Tall cut flower and visual feature; this cultivar is recorded as pollen-free.
