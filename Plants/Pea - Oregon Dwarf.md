# Pea - Oregon Dwarf

![[localdata/plant_images/27-pea-oregon-dwarf.jpg]]

## Summary

Dwarf Oregon snow pea producing edible flat pods; direct sow in cool conditions and provide support.

## Sowing

Feb–Sep

## Care

Grow in well-drained soil with a short trellis or other support.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

0.25 kg per established plant over a 4-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.
