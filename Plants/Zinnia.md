# Zinnia

![[localdata/plant_images/34-zinnia.jpg]]

## Summary

Warm-season annual flower grown for colourful blooms, pollinators and cut flowers.

## Sowing

Sep–Dec

## Care

Grow in full sun and well-drained soil; water regularly and deadhead to extend flowering.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

8 flower per established plant over a 8-week harvest window. Total across repeated pickings, not the yield at every picking.
