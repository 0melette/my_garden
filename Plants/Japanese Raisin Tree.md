# Japanese Raisin Tree

![[localdata/plant_images/22-japanese-raisin-tree.jpg]]

## Summary

Deciduous tree whose small dry fruit is inedible but whose swollen fruit stalks, or peduncles, are edible when ripe.

## Sowing

UNVERIFIED AI PLANNING GUESS: Try the months in planting_months as a rough Sydney starting point; use seed/transplants for annual crops and nursery plants or divisions for perennials. Adjust to weather and establishment.

## Care

Grow in full sun to partial shade in well-drained soil; sandy loam is preferred.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

AI-assisted research, 23 October 2026 (species guidance; needs human review): The sweet edible portion is the swollen stalk/receptacle rather than the small seed capsule. Ripening and local harvest dates need confirmation. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hovenia-dulcis/).

## Uses

Unusual deciduous specimen tree with edible swollen fruit stalks.
Ripe swollen peduncles can be eaten fresh or dried.
