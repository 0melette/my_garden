# Japanese Raisin Tree

![[localdata/plant_images/22-japanese-raisin-tree.jpg]]

## Summary

Deciduous tree whose small dry fruit is inedible but whose swollen fruit stalks, or peduncles, are edible when ripe.

## Sowing

<!-- Not recorded. Add verified guidance here. -->

## Care

Grow in full sun to partial shade in well-drained soil; sandy loam is preferred.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

<!-- Not recorded. Add verified guidance here. -->

## Uses

Unusual deciduous specimen tree with edible swollen fruit stalks.
Ripe swollen peduncles can be eaten fresh or dried.
