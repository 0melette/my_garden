# Okra

![[localdata/plant_images/36-okra.jpg]]

## Summary

Heat-loving annual vegetable grown for tender immature pods.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: sow October–December after frost risk, when soil is warm. Sow about 1 cm deep. Calendar and spacing are general okra guidance, not a named-variety trial.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Use a warm, sunny, drained bed. Space 60 cm apart in rows 120 cm apart, the wider end of general okra guidance. Water steadily; excess nitrogen encourages leaves over pods. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, feeder type, water needs.

## Management

Mulch and keep drainage open. Inspect leaf undersides and remove badly affected material after identifying the problem. The light-feeder badge means avoid excessive nitrogen, not omit all fertiliser.

## Harvest

Pick tender immature pods frequently before they become fibrous. First picking is generally around 80–98 days from sowing; this is maturity time, not harvest-window length. Yield weight varies.

## Uses

Cook young pods in curries, soups or stews, or roast them. Harvest with care where stems or pods are prickly.
