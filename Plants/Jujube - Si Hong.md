# Jujube - Si Hong

![[localdata/plant_images/44-jujube-si-hong.jpg]]

## Summary

Si Hong is a jujube listed by Daleys for large sweet fruit used fresh or dried. Supplier spelling: Si Hong; also commonly written Sihong.

## Sowing

Daleys supplies grafted plants. Sydney planting months are not recorded.

## Care

Scope: Supplier-listed Si Hong information, checked 20 October 2026; individual plant identity is not independently verified.

Sunlight: Full sun. Soil: good drainage; listed pH 6.6–7.3. Daleys describes drought hardiness, not a fixed watering interval. Watering frequency and fertiliser dose are not recorded here.

Pollination: Daleys lists self-pollination but recommends cross-pollination for best results.

Containers: Supplier lists suitability for pots and repotting every second year while deciduous. Pruning technique is not recorded here.

Watering: Species guidance favours moist, well-drained soil despite established drought tolerance. Drought tolerance is not a recommendation to withhold establishment watering. [Source](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ziziphus-jujuba/).

## Management

Daleys warns about rootstock suckering and advises avoiding disturbance around the roots. Pest and disease associations are not recorded.

## Harvest

Supplier describes mid-season ripening and lists March–May harvest. Local timing varies; no yield estimate is recorded.

## Uses

Fresh or dried fruit.
