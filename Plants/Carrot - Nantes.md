# Carrot - Nantes

![[localdata/plant_images/15-carrot-nantes.jpg]]

## Summary

Cylindrical carrot suited to loose, well-prepared soil or a deep container.

## Sowing

Feb–Nov

## Care

Use loose soil and thin seedlings to their final spacing.

## Management

Inspect weekly, remove affected plant material, use clean tools and encourage beneficial insects. Confirm the pest or disease before choosing treatment.

## Harvest

1 root per established plant over a 1-week harvest window. Harvest each plant once during this picking period.

## Uses

Root crop suited to a deep container with loose soil.
Raw, roasted, steamed, in soups or in baking.
