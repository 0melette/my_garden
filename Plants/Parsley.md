# Parsley

![[localdata/plant_images/35-parsley.jpg]]

## Summary

Leafy biennial culinary herb, available in curly and flat-leaved forms.

## Sowing

Sydney / warm temperate: sow September–May (Eden mild-climate guide). Sow 0.5–1 cm deep and keep moist; germination can be slow. Calendar refers to seed sowing.

## Care

Sydney / warm-temperate guidance. Grow in sun or partial shade with moist, drained soil. The 30 cm plant / 60 cm row spacing uses the wider end of the supplier range. Add compost; feed only as needed. Planning estimates: in row spacing cm, row spacing cm, feeder type, water needs.

## Management

Protect seedlings from slugs and snails. Allow space and airflow; avoid keeping leaves wet overnight. Feeding and water badges are qualitative care categories, not measured requirements.

## Harvest

Harvest outer stems once established, leaving the centre to regrow. Seed-grown plants generally reach first harvest in about 65–135 days. A reliable per-plant weight is not established.

## Uses

Use fresh leaves in salads, sauces and cooked dishes; freeze or dry surplus. Retain a separate plant for flowering or seed if desired.
