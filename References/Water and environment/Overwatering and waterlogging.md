# Overwatering and waterlogging

## Description

Wilting, yellow leaves and persistently wet soil can accompany roots deprived of air. Check: Check soil moisture, drainage holes and standing water before adding more water. First actions: Allow appropriate drying, empty standing water and improve drainage; follow the plant’s moisture needs. Similar-looking problems: Underwatering; root rot; nutrient problems. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Wilting, yellow leaves and persistently wet soil can accompany roots deprived of air.

## Similar-looking problems

Underwatering; root rot; nutrient problems.

## Checks to make

Check soil moisture, drainage holes and standing water before adding more water.

## First actions and prevention

Allow appropriate drying, empty standing water and improve drainage; follow the plant’s moisture needs. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
