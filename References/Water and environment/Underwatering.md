# Underwatering

## Description

Wilting, dry growing medium and crisp leaf edges can occur. Check: Check moisture below the surface and the root ball, including whether water runs around dry compost. First actions: Water the root zone thoroughly when needed, allow drainage and reassess moisture before repeating. Similar-looking problems: Overwatering; heat stress; root damage. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Wilting, dry growing medium and crisp leaf edges can occur.

## Similar-looking problems

Overwatering; heat stress; root damage.

## Checks to make

Check moisture below the surface and the root ball, including whether water runs around dry compost.

## First actions and prevention

Water the root zone thoroughly when needed, allow drainage and reassess moisture before repeating. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
