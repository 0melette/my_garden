# Frost damage

## Description

Darkened, collapsed or damaged soft growth can follow cold weather. Check: Check recent temperatures and which exposed tissues were affected. First actions: Protect vulnerable plants and avoid immediate heavy pruning until damage and new growth are clear. Similar-looking problems: Root problems; infectious rot. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Darkened, collapsed or damaged soft growth can follow cold weather.

## Similar-looking problems

Root problems; infectious rot.

## Checks to make

Check recent temperatures and which exposed tissues were affected.

## First actions and prevention

Protect vulnerable plants and avoid immediate heavy pruning until damage and new growth are clear. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
