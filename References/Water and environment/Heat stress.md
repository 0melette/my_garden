# Heat stress

## Description

Temporary wilting or scorch may coincide with hot, drying weather. Check: Check temperature, soil moisture and whether the plant recovers in cooler conditions. First actions: Provide suitable temporary shade and shelter and maintain appropriate soil moisture. Similar-looking problems: Underwatering; sunburn; root damage. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Temporary wilting or scorch may coincide with hot, drying weather.

## Similar-looking problems

Underwatering; sunburn; root damage.

## Checks to make

Check temperature, soil moisture and whether the plant recovers in cooler conditions.

## First actions and prevention

Provide suitable temporary shade and shelter and maintain appropriate soil moisture. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
