# Sunburn

## Description

Bleached or scorched patches may appear after sudden exposure to stronger sun. Check: Check whether damage is concentrated on exposed surfaces after a light change. First actions: Acclimatise plants gradually to stronger light; protect sensitive plants during extreme exposure. Similar-looking problems: Disease spots; fertiliser damage. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Bleached or scorched patches may appear after sudden exposure to stronger sun.

## Similar-looking problems

Disease spots; fertiliser damage.

## Checks to make

Check whether damage is concentrated on exposed surfaces after a light change.

## First actions and prevention

Acclimatise plants gradually to stronger light; protect sensitive plants during extreme exposure. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
