# Powdery mildew

## Description

A fungal disease recognised by white or grey powdery patches on leaves and stems; airflow and spacing help reduce it.

## Visible signs

White or grey powdery patches; severely affected leaves may yellow or distort.

## Checks to make

Inspect both leaf surfaces and confirm the host-specific problem before treatment.

## First actions and prevention

Improve spacing and airflow, remove badly affected material and choose resistant varieties when available. Companion planting does not cure this disease.
