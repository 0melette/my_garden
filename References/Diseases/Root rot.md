# Root rot

## Description

Poor growth and wilting can accompany discoloured, decaying roots. Check: Check drainage and roots where this can be done without serious damage; symptoms alone cannot identify a pathogen. First actions: Correct drainage and watering; isolate affected pots and seek host-specific guidance. Similar-looking problems: Waterlogging without infection; underwatering. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Poor growth and wilting can accompany discoloured, decaying roots.

## Similar-looking problems

Waterlogging without infection; underwatering.

## Checks to make

Check drainage and roots where this can be done without serious damage; symptoms alone cannot identify a pathogen.

## First actions and prevention

Correct drainage and watering; isolate affected pots and seek host-specific guidance. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
