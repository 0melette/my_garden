# Grey mould

## Description

Grey fuzzy growth and decay can affect flowers, leaves and fruit. Check: Check damaged tissue and persistently damp areas; confirm the host and pattern. First actions: Remove decaying material, improve airflow and avoid prolonged wet foliage. Similar-looking problems: Other rots; ageing flowers. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Grey fuzzy growth and decay can affect flowers, leaves and fruit.

## Similar-looking problems

Other rots; ageing flowers.

## Checks to make

Check damaged tissue and persistently damp areas; confirm the host and pattern.

## First actions and prevention

Remove decaying material, improve airflow and avoid prolonged wet foliage. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
