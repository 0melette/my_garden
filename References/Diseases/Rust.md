# Rust

## Description

Raised orange, brown or yellow pustules may appear on leaves. Check: Inspect the underside and check a host-specific rust reference. First actions: Remove heavily affected leaves and improve airflow; avoid spreading infected material. Similar-looking problems: Other leaf spots; insect eggs. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Raised orange, brown or yellow pustules may appear on leaves.

## Similar-looking problems

Other leaf spots; insect eggs.

## Checks to make

Inspect the underside and check a host-specific rust reference.

## First actions and prevention

Remove heavily affected leaves and improve airflow; avoid spreading infected material. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
