# Downy mildew

## Description

Angular yellow patches and growth beneath leaves may occur; appearance varies by host. Check: Inspect both leaf surfaces and identify the host before choosing management. First actions: Reduce prolonged leaf wetness, improve spacing and remove badly affected material. Similar-looking problems: Powdery mildew; nutrient problems. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Angular yellow patches and growth beneath leaves may occur; appearance varies by host.

## Similar-looking problems

Powdery mildew; nutrient problems.

## Checks to make

Inspect both leaf surfaces and identify the host before choosing management.

## First actions and prevention

Reduce prolonged leaf wetness, improve spacing and remove badly affected material. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
