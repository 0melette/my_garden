# Damping-off

## Description

Seedlings fail to emerge or collapse with decay near soil level. Check: Inspect the stem base, moisture and whether tissue is rotting or chewed. First actions: Use clean containers and fresh suitable seed compost; provide drainage and avoid crowding. Similar-looking problems: Dry soil; cutworm or slug damage. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Seedlings fail to emerge or collapse with decay near soil level.

## Similar-looking problems

Dry soil; cutworm or slug damage.

## Checks to make

Inspect the stem base, moisture and whether tissue is rotting or chewed.

## First actions and prevention

Use clean containers and fresh suitable seed compost; provide drainage and avoid crowding. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
