# Tomato early blight

## Description

Brown leaf spots with concentric rings can develop, often on older tomato leaves. Check: Inspect spot patterns and compare a tomato-specific reference; do not identify from yellowing alone. First actions: Remove affected debris, reduce soil splash and provide spacing and crop rotation. Similar-looking problems: Septoria leaf spot; other blights; nutrient problems. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Brown leaf spots with concentric rings can develop, often on older tomato leaves.

## Similar-looking problems

Septoria leaf spot; other blights; nutrient problems.

## Checks to make

Inspect spot patterns and compare a tomato-specific reference; do not identify from yellowing alone.

## First actions and prevention

Remove affected debris, reduce soil splash and provide spacing and crop rotation. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
