# Caterpillars

## Description

Chewed leaves, holes and droppings can accompany caterpillars. Check: Inspect both surfaces and nearby shelter; identify the caterpillar and host. First actions: Hand-pick where practical and use suitable exclusion; distinguish pest species from desirable butterflies. Similar-looking problems: Slugs; beetles; other chewing pests. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Chewed leaves, holes and droppings can accompany caterpillars.

## Similar-looking problems

Slugs; beetles; other chewing pests.

## Checks to make

Inspect both surfaces and nearby shelter; identify the caterpillar and host.

## First actions and prevention

Hand-pick where practical and use suitable exclusion; distinguish pest species from desirable butterflies. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
