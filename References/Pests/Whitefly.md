# Whitefly

## Description

Small pale flying insects beneath leaves; honeydew may occur. Check: Inspect undersides and gently disturb foliage to check for adults. First actions: Monitor regularly; encourage beneficial insects and use host-appropriate controls. Similar-looking problems: Aphids; scale; leaf debris. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Small pale flying insects beneath leaves; honeydew may occur.

## Similar-looking problems

Aphids; scale; leaf debris.

## Checks to make

Inspect undersides and gently disturb foliage to check for adults.

## First actions and prevention

Monitor regularly; encourage beneficial insects and use host-appropriate controls. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
