# Thrips

## Description

Silvery scarring or distorted growth may accompany tiny slender insects. Check: Inspect flowers and leaves with a hand lens; look for insects and dark waste spots. First actions: Monitor and remove badly affected material; conserve beneficial insects. Similar-looking problems: Spider mites; wind damage. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Silvery scarring or distorted growth may accompany tiny slender insects.

## Similar-looking problems

Spider mites; wind damage.

## Checks to make

Inspect flowers and leaves with a hand lens; look for insects and dark waste spots.

## First actions and prevention

Monitor and remove badly affected material; conserve beneficial insects. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
