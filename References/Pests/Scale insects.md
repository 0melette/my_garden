# Scale insects

## Description

Attached bumps on stems or leaves may accompany poor growth or honeydew. Check: Check whether bumps are insects and identify the scale type. First actions: Remove small infestations gently where appropriate; conserve predators and seek species-specific advice. Similar-looking problems: Plant tissue; mealybugs; other insects. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Attached bumps on stems or leaves may accompany poor growth or honeydew.

## Similar-looking problems

Plant tissue; mealybugs; other insects.

## Checks to make

Check whether bumps are insects and identify the scale type.

## First actions and prevention

Remove small infestations gently where appropriate; conserve predators and seek species-specific advice. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
