# Aphids

## Description

Small sap-sucking insects that gather on tender shoots and beneath leaves, sometimes causing curled growth and sticky honeydew.

## Visible signs

Clusters of small insects on tender growth, curled leaves and sticky honeydew.

## Checks to make

Check fresh growth and both leaf surfaces; identify the insects before treatment.

## First actions and prevention

Dislodge small colonies with a suitable water spray and encourage beneficial insects. Monitor fresh growth.
