# Spider mites

## Description

Fine stippling and sometimes delicate webbing, often in warm dry conditions. Check: Use a hand lens on leaf undersides; confirm mites before treatment. First actions: Reduce plant stress and conserve predators; use suitable controls only after identification. Similar-looking problems: Thrips; nutrient problems. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Fine stippling and sometimes delicate webbing, often in warm dry conditions.

## Similar-looking problems

Thrips; nutrient problems.

## Checks to make

Use a hand lens on leaf undersides; confirm mites before treatment.

## First actions and prevention

Reduce plant stress and conserve predators; use suitable controls only after identification. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
