# Leaf spots

A symptom is an observation, not a diagnosis. These are possible explanations, not an exhaustive list. Natural ageing and plant-specific conditions may also matter.

## Possible causes

- [[References/Diseases/Tomato early blight]]
- [[References/Diseases/Rust]]
- [[References/Diseases/Downy mildew]]
- [[References/Water and environment/Sunburn]]

## Checks to make

Record the plant, affected leaf age, distribution of damage, soil moisture, recent weather and feeding. Inspect both leaf surfaces and compare the linked guides before acting.
