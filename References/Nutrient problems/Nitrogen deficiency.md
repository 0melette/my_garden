# Nitrogen deficiency

## Description

General pale growth, often beginning in older leaves, can suggest low nitrogen. Check: Check leaf age, growth, feeding history and root conditions; consider a soil or growing-medium test. First actions: Correct the cause and use an appropriate balanced feed if deficiency is supported. Similar-looking problems: Root stress; low light; natural ageing. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

General pale growth, often beginning in older leaves, can suggest low nitrogen.

## Similar-looking problems

Root stress; low light; natural ageing.

## Checks to make

Check leaf age, growth, feeding history and root conditions; consider a soil or growing-medium test.

## First actions and prevention

Correct the cause and use an appropriate balanced feed if deficiency is supported. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
