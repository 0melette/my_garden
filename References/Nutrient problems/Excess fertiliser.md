# Excess fertiliser

## Description

Leaf-tip or edge scorch and impaired growth may follow heavy feeding. Check: Review feed concentration, frequency, drainage and any salt crust. First actions: Pause excess feeding and follow product and plant guidance; correct drainage before any leaching. Similar-looking problems: Drought; nutrient shortage; sunburn. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Leaf-tip or edge scorch and impaired growth may follow heavy feeding.

## Similar-looking problems

Drought; nutrient shortage; sunburn.

## Checks to make

Review feed concentration, frequency, drainage and any salt crust.

## First actions and prevention

Pause excess feeding and follow product and plant guidance; correct drainage before any leaching. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
