# Potassium deficiency

## Description

Older leaf margins may yellow or scorch, with poor growth. Check: Check moisture, fertiliser history and soil test results before assuming potassium shortage. First actions: Use an appropriate feed when supported by evidence; avoid excessive potassium. Similar-looking problems: Drought; salt damage; other deficiencies. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Older leaf margins may yellow or scorch, with poor growth.

## Similar-looking problems

Drought; salt damage; other deficiencies.

## Checks to make

Check moisture, fertiliser history and soil test results before assuming potassium shortage.

## First actions and prevention

Use an appropriate feed when supported by evidence; avoid excessive potassium. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
