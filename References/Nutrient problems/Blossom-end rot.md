# Blossom-end rot

## Description

A dark, sunken patch develops at the blossom end of susceptible fruit. Check: Inspect the location and review fluctuating soil moisture; this is a physiological disorder. First actions: Maintain even appropriate moisture; adding calcium alone may not solve impaired calcium transport. Similar-looking problems: Infectious fruit rots; sunscald. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

A dark, sunken patch develops at the blossom end of susceptible fruit.

## Similar-looking problems

Infectious fruit rots; sunscald.

## Checks to make

Inspect the location and review fluctuating soil moisture; this is a physiological disorder.

## First actions and prevention

Maintain even appropriate moisture; adding calcium alone may not solve impaired calcium transport. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
