# Iron deficiency

## Description

Young leaves may yellow between veins while veins remain greener. Check: Check the youngest leaves, growing-medium pH and whether the plant needs acidic conditions. First actions: Address unsuitable pH or root conditions before adding iron; use plant-appropriate products. Similar-looking problems: Other micronutrient problems; damaged roots. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Young leaves may yellow between veins while veins remain greener.

## Similar-looking problems

Other micronutrient problems; damaged roots.

## Checks to make

Check the youngest leaves, growing-medium pH and whether the plant needs acidic conditions.

## First actions and prevention

Address unsuitable pH or root conditions before adding iron; use plant-appropriate products. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
