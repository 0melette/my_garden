# Magnesium deficiency

## Description

Older leaves may yellow between veins. Check: Compare older and younger leaves and check excessive potassium feeding. First actions: Confirm the cause before adding magnesium; avoid routine Epsom-salt treatment. Similar-looking problems: Iron deficiency; root stress; other deficiencies. These signs suggest possibilities, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Visible signs

Older leaves may yellow between veins.

## Similar-looking problems

Iron deficiency; root stress; other deficiencies.

## Checks to make

Compare older and younger leaves and check excessive potassium feeding.

## First actions and prevention

Confirm the cause before adding magnesium; avoid routine Epsom-salt treatment. Monitor new growth and record changes rather than expecting damaged leaves to recover.

## Affected plants

Host susceptibility varies. Confirm guidance for the plant species; this note does not assign every catalogue plant to this problem.
